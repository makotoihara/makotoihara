# Makoto Ihara, Ph.D.

**Professor | Department of Applied Biological Chemistry, Faculty of Agriculture, Kindai University, Japan**

I study the molecular mechanisms of ligand-gated ion channels, with a particular focus on nicotinic acetylcholine receptors (nAChRs) and the selectivity of insecticides targeting these receptors. My research combines experimental and computational approaches to understand receptor function, ligand recognition, and structure–activity relationships.

I am also interested in developing practical, reproducible software tools that make experimental data analysis and molecular simulations more accessible to researchers.

[researchmap](https://researchmap.jp/makoto.ihara) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=OTdwbJ8AAAAJ) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6403-0781) · [Kindai University](https://www.kindai.ac.jp/english/research/researchers/introduce/ihara-makoto-98e.html)

## Research interests

- **Ligand-gated ion channels:** nAChR function, pharmacology, and ligand recognition
- **Pesticide science:** mechanisms of action and species-selective toxicity of insecticides, including neonicotinoids
- **Structural and functional biology:** connecting molecular structures to receptor function
- **Computational chemistry:** protein–ligand modeling and molecular dynamics (MD) simulations
- **Experimental approaches:** electrophysiology and biophysical characterization of protein–ligand interactions
